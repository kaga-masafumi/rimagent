# Early-game food

- `food_days < 2`: emergency; stop optional construction/research, secure survival meals/raw food, and prioritize cooking/growing/hauling through steward posture.
- Confirm food exists with a food report/map search before concluding it is unavailable; drop-pod items may be forbidden or not yet spawned.
- Build a campfire immediately when wood permits and add `CookMealSimple` after construction. Keep the target around 10–20 meals in warm weather; large meal buffers rot quickly without a freezer.
- Plant rice first: roughly 10–25 tiles per colonist depending on skill/season; expand only after food is stable. Shift to corn later when a buffer exists and the season allows.
- Set early hunting targets around 80–120 meat. Prefer deer, elk, boar, and turkey with 0% revenge; never hunt predators, boom animals, or herd species with revenge risk. A large designation can monopolize the only builder.
- If a hunt designation exceeds the stock target, the steward may report target satisfied and stop; reconcile the target/designations rather than repeatedly hand-designating.
- After food reaches 6+ days, finish shelter and defense before optional expansion or missions.
