# Core doctrine

## Priorities
1. Food, 2. shelter/temperature, 3. defense, 4. mood/health, 5. research and wealth. Do not expand or accept missions until food and shelter are stable.

## Day-0 emergency protocol
- Treat `food_days < 2` as an emergency. Immediately locate all survival meals/food, create a stockpile, and unforbid only confirmed spawned items.
- Build a roofed, enclosed shelter with a door and sleeping spots/beds before decorative or research expansion. If wood is scarce, use sleeping spots and the smallest room first.
- Place a campfire/stove and add its cooking bill as soon as it is built; verify the bill, not just the blueprint.
- Plant rice on fertile soil; use hunting only for safe 0%-revenge animals and keep the hunting target modest (about 80–120 meat early) so the best builder is not trapped hunting.
- Use a food posture to bias Cooking/Growing/Construction and reduce Hauling; never let hauling consume every capable worker.
- Check food, shelter temperature, blueprints/frames, bills, and mood after 8–12 in-game hours.

## Expansion and missions
Do not expand the base or accept missions while food is under 6 days, shelter is incomplete, or fewer than one ranged and one melee defense option are ready. Missions are optional wealth/threat multipliers; evaluate reward, travel food, combat risk, and current threat window first.

Record concrete food duration, raid timing/size, deaths, and the earliest preventable failure. Edit this skill only when timeline evidence supports a change.
